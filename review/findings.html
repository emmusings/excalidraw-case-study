Excalidraw case study

What we changed in Hover Motion

Put Image and Keep tool in plain sight. Keep the other labels and shortcuts available on hover and keyboard focus.

Try Hover Motion Back to the toolbar comparison

Top findings

  1. 3 of 6 did not finish the repeat-drawing task. P5: “Keep tool... keep it where?”

    We gave the toggle a visible pin and a tooltip that explains what stays active after drawing.

  2. 1 of 6 missed Image on a tablet; 5 of 6 added a photo. P6: “Image being right there is great. Last time I couldn't find it at all.”

    We moved Image onto the rail and kept 44 px targets even when the toolbar wraps on narrow layouts.

  3. 6 of 6 finished boxes and arrows. P1: “Oh, they have names now. That's all I needed.” P2: “I'd turn the words off after a day, but today I need them.”

    We kept contextual names and shortcuts, turned animation off for reduced motion, and added a dot to mark the selected tool.
